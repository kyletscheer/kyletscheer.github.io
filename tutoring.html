How it works

I help high schoolers applying to university (SAT/ACT) and prospective graduate students (GRE) build clear strategies to reach their target scores. In-person sessions take place around central Warsaw (Śródmieście, Mokotów, cafes/libraries, or client homes), as well as virtually via Zoom.

To ensure every student gets my full attention and a tailored roadmap, I only take 4–5 regular students at a time.

  1. Diagnostic & StrategyWe start with a full diagnostic test to pinpoint high-leverage areas in both Quantitative and Verbal sections.
  2. Custom Study PlanI provide official practice resources and a week-by-week plan built around your busy schedule and target score.
  3. Focused 1-on-1 SessionsWe spend session time dismantling challenge areas, mastering timing strategies, and building confidence to manage test anxiety.

I'm patient, structured, and specialize in working with students balancing heavy course loads, demanding jobs, or test-day nerves.
