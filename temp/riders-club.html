Our story

People helping people, since one Rotary meeting in Dubuque

Riders Club of America started with an idea borrowed and improved. At a Rotary meeting in Dubuque, Iowa, founder Martin Wissenberg heard a presentation on DuRide — a ride-share program built to help people who could no longer drive stay connected to their communities.

Martin thought, "I can do that." Drawing on a background in logistics, he built the scheduling software that still powers the club today, matching volunteer drivers to riders and automating pre-payment so members can simply get in and go.

Within months he'd partnered with senior care providers and city officials across the Cedar Rapids area, and Riders Club of America was on the road.

Step 1

A Rotary presentation on Dubuque's DuRide program sparks the idea.

Step 2

Martin Wissenberg builds a scheduling & pre-payment system from the ground up.

Step 3

Partnerships form with Cedar Rapids senior care providers and city officials.

Step 4

Riders Club of America launches, connecting volunteer drivers and riders 24/7.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What are the requirements to qualify?+
Riders must be 55 or older, or have a medical condition that keeps them from driving.
Is any evaluation needed before I can use the service?+
Every applicant — both drivers and riders — goes through a background check.
Can you accommodate wheelchairs?+
We're not able to accommodate wheelchairs at this time, but members using walkers or canes are welcome.
Can a family member ride along as an escort?+
Yes — a caregiver can ride along with a member at no additional cost.
What does the service cost?+
Membership includes an annual fee plus a prepaid mileage fee for each ride or destination.
Are discounts available?+
A senior discount applies to every ride, automatically.
Does my income affect my eligibility?+
No — income is not currently a factor in eligibility.
How does account prepayment work?+
Rides are paid for through a personal account. We'll reach out when your balance hits the threshold you set (minimum $10), and a minimum $40 recharge keeps your account active.
What's the service area?+
The Cedar Rapids metro area, including Marion and Hiawatha — roughly bounded by Tower Terrace Rd (N), Highway 13 (E), US 30 (S), and Stoney Point Rd (W).
What hours does the service run?+
Rides run 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Our office is staffed 9am–3pm, Monday through Friday, and we can't schedule new rides outside those hours.
How far in advance do I need to book?+
Please let us know by noon the business day before you need a ride — the sooner, the better.
Is it door-to-door service?+
Yes. Door-to-door pickup and drop-off means you can wait comfortably at home until your driver arrives.
Do you go to social visits, or only medical appointments?+
Both, and everything in between — we'll take you wherever you need to go.
Can the driver wait while I'm inside?+
Drivers may have other rides scheduled after yours, but can wait up to 15 minutes as their schedule allows.
Can my pet come along?+
Contact the office ahead of time so we can line up a driver able to transport a secured pet.
Will the driver help carry packages?+
Our drivers are glad to help however they're able to.
